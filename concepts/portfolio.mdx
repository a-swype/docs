The portfolio answers the three questions every investor asks: how much did I put in, how much have I made, and what is it worth now. It answers them the same way for every instrument.

## The summary

```json
{
  "total_value_usd": "998.45",
  "net_deposits_usd": "1000.00",
  "total_return_usd": "-1.55",
  "total_return_pct": "-0.16",
  "total_principal_usd": "999.68",
  "total_yield_earned_usd": "0.00",
  "total_unrealized_pnl_usd": "-1.23",
  "total_realized_pnl_usd": "0.00",
  "display": {
    "currency": "BRL",
    "fx_rate": "5.5539",
    "total_value": "5545.27",
    "total_return": "-8.63"
  }
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `total_value_usd` | What everything is worth right now, cash included. |
| `net_deposits_usd` | Deposits minus withdrawals. The money the user put in. |
| `total_return_usd` | Total value minus net deposits. The all-time result, after fees. |
| `total_yield_earned_usd` | Interest earned on savings and yield instruments. |
| `total_unrealized_pnl_usd` | Gain or loss on what the user still holds. |
| `total_realized_pnl_usd` | Gain or loss locked in by selling. |

The headline number for most apps is `total_return_usd`: "You've made $42.10."

## Positions

Each holding is one position, however it was bought and however many purchases built it.

Different kinds of asset are measured the way users think about them:

- **Stocks, gold and crypto** have a cost basis and a profit or loss. Cost basis is the average price paid.
- **Savings and yield** have a principal and yield earned, reported separately. The user sees "$300 deposited, $4.12 earned" without you doing any maths.
- **Cash** has `units` and `available_units`. The difference is money reserved for open orders.

## Display currency

Every value comes in US dollars and in the user's `display_currency`. Pass `?currency=MXN` to override it for one request. The `display.fx_rate` used is included, so your numbers always reconcile.

## History

[`GET /v1/users/{id}/portfolio/history`](/api-reference/portfolio/history) returns a time series ready to chart: total value, net deposits and cumulative yield at each point, for `1D`, `1W`, `1M`, `1Y` or `ALL`.

Plot `value_usd` against `net_deposits_usd` and the gap between the two lines is the user's return.

## Activity

[`GET /v1/users/{id}/activity`](/api-reference/portfolio/activity) is the user's ledger: deposits, buys, sells and withdrawals, newest first. Each entry has a `description` written for end users, such as "Deposit of 1000 USDC".

## Keeping your screen fresh

Listen for `portfolio.updated` [events](/guides/events) and refetch the portfolio when one arrives, or refetch when the user opens the screen. Prices move continuously, so `as_of` tells you when the snapshot was taken.
