Fissient is an invest-and-earn backend for fintechs, wallets and AI agents. Your users buy US stocks, gold, dollar savings and crypto in dollar amounts, and you show them one portfolio. You integrate a single REST API. We handle everything underneath it.

The API speaks in users, dollars, instruments and portfolios. You never manage networks, fees for moving money, or the mechanics of how an asset is bought. An order for $50 of NVIDIA is one call, and so is the portfolio that shows what it's worth.

## What you don't have to build

| You call | We take care of |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /v1/users` | Opening an account for the user and checking where they're allowed to invest |
| `POST /v1/quotes` | Finding the best price or rate and returning one all-in cost |
| `POST /v1/orders` | Converting the user's cash, executing the trade and settling it into their account |
| `GET /v1/users/{id}/portfolio` | Valuing every holding, separating principal from yield, and tracking profit and loss |

## Who can use it

Fissient serves users outside the United States. US residents and US nationals are refused when you create the user, and each instrument has its own country rules on top of that. Every refusal comes back with a machine-readable reason, so your app can explain it. See [Eligibility](/concepts/eligibility).
